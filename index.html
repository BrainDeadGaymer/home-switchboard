Set up in about 10 minutes

The app walks you through every step with pictures.

  1. Create a free Tuya developer accountTuya is the platform behind the Smart Life app. The account is free.
  2. Link your Smart Life appScan a QR code with the Smart Life app on your phone to connect your devices.
  3. Paste your two keysCopy your Access ID and Secret into Home Switchboard, and your devices appear.
Home Switchboard controls devices you've already added with the Smart Life app on your phone. It can't add new devices: set those up in the phone app first, then click Refresh.
